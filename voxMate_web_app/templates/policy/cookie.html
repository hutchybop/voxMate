Cookie Policy

  • What Are Cookies?

    Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites that you visit. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the site owners.

  • How We Use Cookies

    Our website uses essential cookies that are necessary for the operation of the site. These cookies enable core functionality such as security, network management, and accessibility. You may disable these by changing your browser settings, but this may affect how the website functions.

    voxmate_session
    Purpose:
    This session cookie is used to manage your login state and ensure secure access to the web app features, including updating voxMate settings and configuring Spotify integration.
    Duration:

    Local Storage:
    We also use local storage to enhance your experience by remembering whether you have seen the cookie consent alert.
    Key:
    'hasVisited'
    Purpose:
    This boolean value is used to determine whether to show the cookie consent alert to users. It helps in ensuring that you are informed about our cookie usage only once, improving your user experience.

    We do not use any third-party cookies or tracking services. No cookie data or personal information is shared with external providers.

    By continuing to use our website, you consent to the use of these essential cookies. These cookies are necessary for the functionality of the website, and opting out may hinder your ability to use certain features.

  • Managing Cookies

    Since the cookies we use are essential for the functionality of the website, we do not provide an option to disable them through our site. However, you can manage cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies in your browser may affect the functionality of our website.

    We may update our Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Please review this policy regularly to stay informed about our use of cookies.
